# Handshake Privacy Policy

Last updated: September, 2026

Handshake is a Chrome extension that lets you carry your conversation from one AI chat website into another with a single click. This policy explains, in plain language, what the extension does, what data it touches, and how that data is handled.

## The short version

Handshake does not have accounts, servers, or a database of its own. Everything Handshake saves stays inside your own browser, on your own device. Handshake never sends your conversations anywhere over the internet. Handshake does not use ads, analytics, or tracking of any kind. Handshake does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, because it never leaves your device in the first place.

## What Handshake does

Handshake adds a small button to supported AI chat websites (currently ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini). Clicking it does one of two things.

On the chat you are copying from, it reads the messages in that conversation and saves a copy of them, on your device only. On the chat you are pasting into, it takes that saved copy and types it into the message box for you. It never sends the message on your behalf. You always have to press send yourself.

That is the entire purpose of the extension. It does not summarize, analyze, or send your conversations to any outside service, AI model, or third party. It simply moves text you already wrote, along with the AI's replies to it, from one browser tab to another.

## What data Handshake accesses

To do the above, Handshake needs to read the visible content of the AI chat page you are on, meaning your messages and the AI's replies, only on the three supported websites, and only while you are actively using the extension on those pages. It does not read anything on any other website.

Handshake does not access, and has no way to access, any of the following:

Your browsing history on other sites. Your passwords, payment information, or account details. Anything you type outside of the supported AI chat websites. Your webcam, microphone, location, or any other device data.

## Where your data is stored

Everything Handshake saves, meaning the messages it captures and a small marker noting what has already been delivered to a given chat, is stored using your browser's own local storage. This data stays on your device. It is never uploaded, synced to a server, or transmitted anywhere. It is only readable by the Handshake extension itself, not by websites or other extensions. It is automatically deleted about an hour after you close the tab it came from, so nothing lingers indefinitely. It can also be deleted by you at any time, instantly, using the extension's own reset action.

If you uninstall the extension, this data is removed along with it.

## Clipboard access

Handshake requests permission to write to your clipboard. This is used only as a fallback. If the extension cannot find a chat's message box to insert text into directly, it copies the text to your clipboard instead, so you can paste it in yourself. Handshake never reads your existing clipboard contents, and only ever writes the same context you asked it to carry over.

## Why Handshake needs the permissions it asks for

Chrome requires extensions to state plainly what they can access. Here is what Handshake asks for and why.

Access to ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini pages is needed to read and insert messages on those specific sites. Handshake cannot see or act on any other website.

Local storage is needed to temporarily hold the copied context on your device between the two chats.

Alarms are needed only to schedule the automatic cleanup described above, which happens about an hour after a source tab closes. This is not used for anything else, and does not wake up your browser or run in the background beyond that.

Clipboard write is used only for the fallback described above.

Handshake does not request, and does not need, permission to access the internet at large, other websites, your browsing history, or any other browser data.

## No accounts, no sign in

Handshake does not have user accounts. There is nothing to sign up for, and no personal information such as your name or email is ever collected or requested by the extension itself.

## Children's privacy

Handshake is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since it does not collect data at all in the sense of sending it anywhere. Everything described above stays local to your device.

## Changes to this policy

If Handshake's data practices ever change, for example if a future version adds an optional feature that involves an outside service, this policy will be updated to reflect that clearly before such a feature becomes available.
